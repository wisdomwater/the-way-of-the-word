## 26 – The Stillness That Travels With You

High in the mountains, bamboo moves wildly beneath the passing wind, yet somewhere below the soil its roots remain in darkness, holding quietly to the earth. The branches may dance; the root does not need to follow them. Perhaps we also require such hidden weight. The world offers a thousand bright horizons, each whispering that life is somewhere other than here. We hurry toward one view and then another, carrying our restlessness from place to place. Yet the Sage can travel a hundred miles without abandoning home, because home has become something within her. She walks through crowded markets and along empty ridges with the same quiet center. Clouds gather and scatter. Roads rise and descend. Faces appear and disappear. Still, beneath all this movement, there is a place in her that has learned not to move.

I think of the Carpenter asleep in a small boat while the lake rose around him. Wind pulled at the sail, waves struck the wood, frightened voices filled the darkness—and still he rested. Perhaps the greater wonder was not that the storm became quiet, but that quietness already lived within him before the waters were stilled. There is a sanctuary like this hidden within us, though we often travel far before discovering it. The Beloved does not wait at the end of some distant road. Beneath our plans, anxieties, ambitions, and wandering thoughts, the Presence remains like deep earth beneath restless grass. We may learn to return there even while our hands continue their work. Then movement need not become hurry, and change need not carry us away. We can travel wherever love calls while remaining rooted in the stillness that never left us.

> *Beloved, root me in the stillness beneath all things.*
