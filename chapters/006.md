## 6 – The Hidden Womb

Before dawn, I often walk to a quiet garden where the mist gathers low among the bamboo and the lilies lift their faces to the coming light. Nothing there appears hurried. The earth carries seeds through the long silence of winter without announcing the work it is doing. Streams emerge from places no eye has seen, and birds awaken as though the morning itself had remembered their names. I have come to trust these hidden beginnings more than the noise of sudden accomplishments. Life seems to delight in growing from places that appear empty. What looks like stillness is often a quiet labor, preparing blossoms that only another season will reveal.

So it has been with the soul. The deepest movements of the Beloved have seldom arrived with thunder. More often they have come like bread rising beneath a linen cloth, like oil filling a small lamp before nightfall, like a shepherd calling so gently that only the listening heart turns its head. There is within us a spacious place that neither fear nor failure can exhaust. I cannot say where it begins, only that whenever I grow still enough to enter it, I discover I have not come alone. The Friend has been waiting there all along, nurturing unseen life with the patience of One who has never forgotten how to bring light from hidden places.

> *Awaken the hidden life within me.*
