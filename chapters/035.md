## 35 – The Quiet Beneath the Song

Walk through a village at dusk and you will know what draws the senses. A flute sounds through an open window. Ginger and garlic rise from a cooking fire. Lanterns glow beside the road, and travelers slow their steps. The Way is rarely so noticeable. Look for it and there is no bright object to behold. Listen, and no melody announces its presence. Yet beneath the music, beneath the fragrance, beneath laughter and grief alike, something quiet remains. The Sage has learned to rest there. This does not spare her from pain. Storms still cross the valley; beloved faces still disappear from the table. But somewhere within her is a depth the storm cannot entirely disturb. Perhaps peace is not a life without waves, but the discovery that beneath every wave the ocean remains whole—silent, immeasurable, carrying everything.

The Shepherd spoke of treasures hidden in fields and kingdoms arriving without spectacle. He often seemed to point toward what could easily be missed: a mustard seed, a widow's coin, yeast disappearing into dough, lilies opening where no one had planted them. The Holy does not always dazzle. Sometimes it is so near that our searching passes over it. We wait for thunder while the Beloved comes as breath. We seek certainty while bread is being broken before us. We strain to hear a voice and overlook the silence in which every voice appears. Perhaps prayer ripens when we stop demanding that God become interesting. We sit. We breathe. We remain. Nothing remarkable may happen, and still the hidden spring continues flowing. Cup after cup may be drawn from it, and the water does not diminish. The quiet Presence asks only to be received.

> *Let me rest in the quiet that never runs dry.*
