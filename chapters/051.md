## 51 – The Love Within All Things

A sparrow does not study how to become a sparrow. The pine does not awaken wondering whether it has earned another season of sunlight. Each simply receives its life and allows wind, rain, hunger, winter, and spring to shape what it becomes. The Sage looks upon the world and sees countless expressions of one hidden generosity. The Way gives birth without signing its name upon what is born. It nourishes roots beneath the soil, carries clouds across the mountains, shelters the small creature beneath a fallen leaf, and asks nothing to bow in gratitude. Everything is allowed to become itself. Nothing is clutched. Even when a life reaches its end, the earth receives it again, gathering leaf, feather, bone, and blossom back into the great turning. Perhaps this is why the world seems to lean naturally toward its Source. The river needs no command to seek the sea.

The Gardener spoke of seeds as though heaven were already hidden inside them. The Shepherd knew each sheep without turning the flock into possessions. The Friend gathered children into his arms, fed hungry crowds, comforted the grieving, and then allowed those he loved to walk their own roads. I sometimes wonder whether divine love is most clearly seen in this strange combination of intimacy and freedom. The Beloved holds us without closing a fist. Grace nourishes without demanding repayment. Even our wandering somehow unfolds within a Presence patient enough to accompany us into distant countries and wait beside every road home. Perhaps our longing for God is not something added to us from outside. Perhaps it is older than thought—the quiet memory of the Source within every creature, drawing all things toward their deepest belonging. The flower turns toward light because something in it already knows the sun.

> *Awaken in me the love that already leans toward You.*
