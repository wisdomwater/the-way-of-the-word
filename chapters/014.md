## 14 – The Unseen Source

The morning mist drifted through the valley before the sun had fully awakened. It softened every ridge without hiding it, wrapped every tree without possessing it. I reached toward it once as a young man, certain I could gather a handful if only I moved quickly enough. My fingers closed upon nothing, yet my face remained cool with its touch. So it has often been with the deepest things. The breeze cannot be seen, yet the bamboo bows before it. The fragrance of pine lingers though no eye can find where it begins. The spring hidden beneath the mountain sends life to fields that have never looked upon its source. Perhaps what is most real has never asked to become an object in our hands, but a quiet presence in which we learn to dwell.

There were evenings beside the lake when the Beloved simply sat among friends as daylight slipped beyond the hills. No one seemed eager to capture the moment or explain the silence. Bread was broken. A small fire crackled. The first stars appeared one by one, unnoticed until someone finally looked up. It may be that the Holy One is known in much the same way—not by chasing after visions, but by becoming still enough to awaken within the life already surrounding us. The mind longs for beginnings and endings, names and definitions, while the heart quietly remembers an older homeland. The Shepherd does not lead us toward a distant country. He walks gently until we discover that every step has always been taken within the embrace of the One who cannot be seen, yet has never ceased to be near.

> *Lead me home to the unseen Presence.*
