## 1 – The Name Beyond Every Name

Before dawn, the valley has no names. The bamboo does not call itself bamboo, nor does the sparrow know it is a sparrow. Mist drifts over the hillside, and the stream follows its ancient course without asking where it should go. The world is content to be itself. Only when we awaken do we begin dividing what has never been separated, giving names to what was already whole. Yet the Holy One is not diminished by our words, nor revealed by them. The Name beyond every name waits patiently beneath them, like the mountain beneath the morning fog.

Walk long enough with the Master and you begin to love the quiet places. There the lilies bloom without striving, the ravens are fed without worry, and seeds disappear into the earth before they rise in glory. The sage smiles at the same mystery. The deeper the roots, the less the tree needs to announce itself. Drink often from this hidden spring. Let your prayers become fewer, your gratitude become deeper, and your heart become spacious enough to welcome the One who has always been nearer than your own breath. In that gentle darkness, where nothing needs to be grasped or explained, every living thing quietly speaks the Name that cannot be spoken.

> *Lead me into the silence where You are already present.*
