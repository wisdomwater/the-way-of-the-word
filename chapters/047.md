## 47 – The World Within

There are journeys measured in miles, and there are journeys made while sitting beside a single window. I once knew an old monk who seldom left the valley where he lived, yet when he spoke, mountains and cities seemed somehow present in his small room. Perhaps he had traveled inward until the walls became thin. We often imagine that understanding waits somewhere beyond us: another book, another teacher, another distant shore. So we gather knowledge like travelers collecting stones, only to find our packs growing heavier while the mystery grows wider. The Sage eventually sits down. She closes the door and listens. In the stillness she discovers that the same Way moving through distant galaxies is breathing quietly within her chest. A single drop reflects the moon. One leaf carries the signature of the forest. We need not possess the whole world to become intimate with its depths.

The Friend also withdrew from crowds into lonely places, as though silence opened a country no road could reach. And he spoke of a kingdom already near, hidden like treasure beneath familiar ground. Perhaps the longest pilgrimage is sometimes the few inches between the mind and the heart. We search beyond the window for the Beloved while Presence waits within the room. This does not make the outer world unimportant. Rather, the deeper we enter the quiet center, the more strangely connected we become to everything beyond ourselves. The hermit in his cell may hold strangers in compassion. The mother beside a sleeping child may touch the same stillness as one praying beneath monastery bells. There comes a knowing that cannot be gathered by looking harder. We close our eyes, release our need to understand, and discover that the Light does not need to be found. It is already shining.

> *Open within me the door that has no distance.*
